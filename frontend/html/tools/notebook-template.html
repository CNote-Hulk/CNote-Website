The concept was born in August 1966, when Ralph H. Baer, an engineer at Sanders Associates, conceived the idea of an interactive device connected to a television. Together with colleagues Bill Harrison and Bill Rusch, Baer built seven successive prototypes between 1966 and 1969. The seventh, named “Brown Box,” included game mechanics and a light gun.
