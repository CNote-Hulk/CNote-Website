We Are

CNote (Console Notebook) is like a hobby dedicated to the world of gaming consoles. It brings together history, technology, and community in one place. But it aims to go beyond documentation. CNote seeks to revive a part of us - the authentic gaming spirit that many lost as the industry changed. Here you can discover consoles, learn how they work, and help preserve an important piece of gaming culture. You're just one button away from starting your own console journey, connecting with fellow enthusiasts, and diving into the rich world of gaming history and technology.
