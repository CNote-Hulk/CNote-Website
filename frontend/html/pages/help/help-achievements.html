Achievements & Progress

Unlocking badges, tracking your level, and understanding progress statistics.

Achievements are badges you earn by reaching milestones on Console Notebook — completing lessons, visiting consoles, making friends, posting in the forum, creating marketplace listings, and more. Each badge also awards XP that goes towards your level. Achievements are shown on the Stats page and on your public profile.
💡 Achievements unlock automatically the moment you reach the threshold — no manual claiming required. A real-time toast notification appears on screen when one unlocks.
There are 30 achievements across six categories: 📝 Learning (7), 🗺️ Explorer (6), 💬 Community (6), 🏷️ Marketplace (4), 📅 Veteran (4), ✨ Special (3).
Achievements unlock automatically. Complete lessons, visit consoles, post in the forum, add friends, create listings, and stay active over time.
Your achievements appear in three places: Home → Achievements panel, the Stats page, and on your public profile (4 most recent badges).
Your level is based on total XP earned from all activity. There are 10 levels from 🔌 Newcomer (0 XP) to 👑 Legend (13 000 XP).
The Stats page shows your XP, level progress bar, all 30 badges by category, lessons completed, consoles visited, friends, and days as a member.

Open the Learn page to browse the course catalogue. Each course is split into modules and lessons, and a lesson can be a video, a text guide, or a quiz. You can leave comments and reactions on any lesson. Completing lessons and courses tracks your progress and awards XP.
💡 Only the Console Starter Guide is fully available today — other listed courses are shown as "Coming Soon" while their content is being written.
The Leaderboard ranks all members by total XP earned, with a podium for the top 3. It is public — no account needed to view it — and highlights your own rank if you are signed in. Open it from the mobile navigation's More menu, or from the link on the home dashboard's Progress panel.
