Profile & Settings

Profile picture, bio, social links, privacy controls, and public profile.

  1. 1
    Go to Settings → Profile.
  2. 2
    Click on your current avatar (or the placeholder circle). A lightbox opens showing your current picture.
  3. 3
    Click Change photo and select an image file from your device.
  4. 4
    The image is saved automatically.
To remove your photo, open the same lightbox and click Remove photo.
💡 The maximum file size is 5 MB. Images are automatically resized to a maximum of 1024 × 1024 pixels and compressed to JPEG before being saved — there is no need to resize them yourself.
Go to Settings → Profile.
  1. 1
    Bio: edit the bio text field. This short description appears on your public profile.
  2. 2
    Social links: fill in any of the four fields — Discord username, Twitter/X handle, YouTube channel URL, and Instagram username.
  3. 3
    Click Save changes.
Social links are visible on your public profile unless you have disabled them in the Privacy section.
Go to Settings → Privacy. You can toggle each option individually:
  1. •
    Show email — off by default. Enable if you want your email visible on your public profile.
  2. •
    Show stats — on by default. Disable to hide your achievements, ratings, and activity counts from other users.
  3. •
    Show friends — on by default. Disable to hide your friends list from other users.
  4. •
    Show social links — on by default. Disable to hide your Discord, Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram links.
Click Save privacy settings after making changes.
💡 Your username, bio, avatar, level badge, console collection, and favourites are always visible — there is no option to make the entire profile completely private.
Your personal stats are available on the Statistics page (accessible from the sidebar or navbar). They include:
  1. •
    Number of achievements unlocked (out of 13 total)
  2. •
    Number of console pages visited
  3. •
    Number of friends
  4. •
    Number of consoles in your favourites
  5. •
    Number of consoles in your collection (owned)
  6. •
    Days as a member
  7. •
    Your current level (Novice → Legend)
On your public profile, other users can see a subset of these stats if the Show stats privacy option is enabled.
💡 Learning stats (lessons, quizzes, courses) are currently under development and will be added when the Learn section is released.
Any user profile is accessible at the URL:

/html/pages/user-profile.html?u=USERNAME

You can also reach a profile by clicking on a username wherever it appears — in the forum, in the chat, in the friends list, or on marketplace listings.
💡 You do not need to be logged in to view public profiles.
The following is always visible to everyone:
  1. •
    Username and avatar
  2. •
    Bio
  3. •
    Member since date
  4. •
    Level badge (Novice → Legend)
  5. •
    Console collection (owned consoles)
  6. •
    Favourite consoles
  7. •
    Active marketplace listings
The following is visible only if the corresponding privacy toggle is enabled:
  1. •
    Email address (Show email)
  2. •
    Stats panel: achievements, ratings, visited consoles (Show stats)
  3. •
    Friends list (Show friends)
  4. •
    Discord, Twitter, YouTube, Instagram links (Show social links)
The date of birth field is used to verify your age. It is not displayed on your public profile and is never shared with other users.
  1. 1
    Go to Settings → Profile.
  2. 2
    Scroll down to the Date of birth row.
  3. 3
    Click the date picker and select your birth date from the calendar. You can click the month/year label to jump to a specific year.
  4. 4
    Click Save changes.
To remove a saved birth date, open the date picker and click Clear, then save.
💡 You must be at least 13 years old to use Console Notebook. The calendar will not allow selecting a date less than 13 years in the past.
