Community & Chat

Community chat, direct messages, and real-time notifications.

Click Community in the navbar. If you are not logged in, you will land on the community welcome page — log in or register to continue. Once inside the community hub, select Chat from the left sidebar.
Yes. You must be logged in to send messages in the community chat. Creating an account is free and only takes a minute.
You can open a DM conversation from a marketplace listing by clicking the contact button on the username panel.
💡 You must be logged in to send DMs.
Go to Community → Direct Messages. The left panel lists all your conversations sorted by most recent message. Click any conversation to open the full message history. Unread conversations are highlighted and show an unread count badge.
Console Notebook uses WebSocket (Socket.IO) to deliver notifications instantly without needing to refresh the page. You receive a real-time notification when:
  1. •
    Someone sends you a friend request or accepts yours.
  2. •
    Someone sends you a direct message.
  3. •
    The support team replies to your repair request.
A white badge with the unread count appears on the bell icon in the navbar. Click it to open the notifications panel.
💡 Notifications are delivered to the specific device and browser tab that is currently open. If you are logged in on multiple devices, each receives its own notification stream.
Open the notifications panel (bell icon in the navbar) and click Mark all as read. All unread notifications are cleared at once. You can also mark individual notifications as read by clicking them.
You can report any piece of content directly from its context — a listing, a forum thread, a forum reply, a chat message, or a user profile — using the Report option (flag icon or three-dot menu).
  1. 1
    Click the report option on the content you want to flag.
  2. 2
    Select a reason: Illegal content, Hate speech, Harassment, Spam, CSAM, Misinformation, or Other.
  3. 3
    Optionally add a description (up to 2 000 characters) and a contact email so the team can follow up.
  4. 4
    Click Submit report. You do not need to be logged in to report.
The Console Notebook moderation team is notified immediately.
💡 You can submit up to 10 reports per hour. This limit applies per IP address regardless of whether you are logged in.
Your submitted reports are listed in Settings → My Reports (you must be logged in). Each report shows:
  1. •
    The content type and a direct link to the reported item (where available).
  2. •
    The reason you selected.
  3. •
    The current status: Pending (received, not yet reviewed), Reviewed (under investigation), Resolved (action taken), or Dismissed (no action needed).
  4. •
    The date the report was submitted.
💡 Only your 20 most recent reports are shown. Anonymous reports (submitted without being logged in) cannot be tracked.
Go to Community → Photos to browse a shared gallery of member-submitted images — console setups, collections, and repairs. Click Upload photo to add your own; you must be signed in, and you can delete photos you uploaded yourself.
