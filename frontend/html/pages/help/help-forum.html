Forum

Creating threads, replying, upvoting, categories, tags, and finding your posts.

  1. 1
    Go to Community → Forum / Ask community.
  2. 2
    Select the category that fits your topic (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, PC, or General).
  3. 3
    Click New thread.
  4. 4
    Enter a title, write your post, and choose a tag.
  5. 5
    Click Post.
💡 You must be logged in to create threads.
There are five categories:
  1. •
    PlayStation — PS1, PS2, PSP, PS3, PS4, PS5, PS Vita.
  2. •
    Xbox — original Xbox, 360, One, Series X/S.
  3. •
    Nintendo — NES, SNES, N64, GameCube, Wii, Switch, Game Boy, DS family.
  4. •
    PC — PC gaming hardware, emulation, and retro computing.
  5. •
    General — anything that doesn't fit the above.
Each thread has one tag that helps users filter content:
  1. •
    General — default tag for general discussion.
  2. •
    Help — asking for help with a problem.
  3. •
    Discussion — open topic for debate or opinions.
  4. •
    News — sharing or discussing news.
  5. •
    Bug — reporting a bug or technical issue.
  6. •
    Guide — step-by-step tutorial or guide.
  7. •
    Modding — console modifications and custom firmware.
Open the thread you want to reply to. Scroll to the bottom and use the reply box. Type your message and click Send. You must be logged in to reply.
Click the ▲ upvote button next to any thread or reply. Upvotes are used to surface the most helpful content. You can upvote each thread and each reply once. You must be logged in to upvote.
Go to Home → My Posts in the dashboard sidebar. This panel lists all threads you have created, sorted by most recent.
Go to Home → Liked Posts in the dashboard sidebar. This panel lists all forum threads you have upvoted.
Keep discussions respectful and on-topic. The general guidelines are:
  1. •
    No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
  2. •
    No spam or repetitive posts.
  3. •
    Post in the correct category and use the appropriate tag.
  4. •
    No piracy links or illegal content.
  5. •
    Search before posting — your question may already be answered.
Violations may result in post removal or account suspension. View the Terms & Conditions for more details.
