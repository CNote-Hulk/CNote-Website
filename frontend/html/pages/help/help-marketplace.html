Marketplace

Posting listings, editing, contacting sellers, favourites, filters, and how transactions work.

  1. 1
    Go to Community → Marketplace.
  2. 2
    Click New listing.
  3. 3
    Fill in the form: title, description, price, condition, category, console type, location (country and city), and optionally a phone number and an OLX link.
  4. 4
    Add up to 8 photos.
  5. 5
    Click Post listing.
The listing goes live immediately and is visible to all users.
💡 You must be logged in to post a listing.
The marketplace has four categories:
  1. •
    Consoles — complete gaming consoles and handheld devices.
  2. •
    Games — physical game cartridges, discs, and digital codes.
  3. •
    Accessories — controllers, cables, memory cards, chargers, and other peripherals.
  4. •
    Parts — individual components intended for repair or modding.
There are five condition levels:
  1. •
    New — unused, in original packaging.
  2. •
    Like new — used very little, no visible wear.
  3. •
    Good — normal signs of use, fully functional.
  4. •
    Fair — visible wear or minor cosmetic damage, still functional.
  5. •
    For parts — not fully functional; sold for repair or parts only.
You can add up to 8 photos per listing. Photos are stored as image URLs — you can use links from image hosting services or upload directly if the interface supports it.
💡 More photos significantly increase interest in a listing. Include shots from multiple angles and any visible wear.
  1. 1
    Open the listing you want to edit (you can find your listings in Home → Collection → My Listings or in your public profile).
  2. 2
    Click the Edit (pencil) button.
  3. 3
    Update any fields and save.
Only the owner of a listing can edit it.
  1. 1
    Find the listing in Home → Collection → My Listings.
  2. 2
    Click the Delete (trash) button on the listing card.
  3. 3
    Confirm the deletion.
Deletion is permanent — the listing cannot be recovered.
In Home → Collection → My Listings, click the ✅ Mark as sold button on the listing card. The listing will display a SOLD badge and be excluded from active search results. You can reverse this by clicking the same button again (it becomes ↩️ Mark as unsold).
On a listing's detail page you can contact the seller in three ways:
  1. 1
    Direct message — send a private message to the seller through the platform's DM system (requires a logged-in account).
  2. 2
    Phone — if the seller provided a phone number, it is shown on the listing.
  3. 3
    OLX link — if the seller linked an external OLX listing, you can view it and contact them there.
On any listing, click the heart (♡) icon to add it to your favourites. Click it again to remove it.

Your saved listings are accessible in Home → Favorites → Favourite Listings.
💡 You must be logged in to save favourites.
In the Marketplace, use the filter panel on the left (or the filter bar on mobile). You can filter by:
  1. •
    Category (Consoles, Games, Accessories, Parts)
  2. •
    Condition
  3. •
    Console type (free text — e.g. "PlayStation 4")
  4. •
    Country
  5. •
    City
  6. •
    Keyword search
All filters are applied server-side — results update when you change the selection.
Use the Sort dropdown in the Marketplace. Available options:
  1. •
    Newest first — most recently posted listings appear at the top (default).
  2. •
    Oldest first — oldest listings appear at the top.
  3. •
    Price: low to high
  4. •
    Price: high to low
Console Notebook is a classifieds platform — it connects buyers and sellers but does not manage or guarantee any transaction. If you believe you have been scammed:
  1. 1
    Report the listing using the Report option on the listing page.
  2. 2
    Contact the Console Notebook team via the contact form with details of the incident.
  3. 3
    For financial disputes, contact your local consumer protection authority or law enforcement — the platform cannot mediate or reverse transactions.
⚠️ Always meet in a public place for in-person transactions. Use secure payment methods with buyer protection (e.g. PayPal Goods & Services) for remote purchases.
No. Console Notebook does not handle, process, or hold any money. The platform is purely a classifieds board — it connects buyers and sellers who then arrange payment and delivery independently. No transaction fees are charged.
Marketplace Connect lets you attach your external OLX profile or eBay username to your Console Notebook account. When set, your listings display a View on OLX or View on eBay button so buyers can find more of your items on those platforms.

To connect your accounts:
  1. 1
    Go to Settings → Profile and scroll to the Marketplace Connect section.
  2. 2
    OLX: paste the full URL of your OLX profile page (e.g. https://www.olx.ro/profile/yourname).
  3. 3
    eBay: enter your eBay username (just the username, not a full URL).
  4. 4
    Click Save changes.
To disconnect, clear the field and save.
💡 Marketplace Connect only adds a link — Console Notebook does not sync listings from OLX or eBay automatically and does not access your accounts on those platforms.
After doing business with a seller, visit their public profile and leave a 1–5 star review with an optional comment. Reviews are tied to the seller, not to a single listing, so they stay visible even after that listing is sold or removed. You can leave one review per seller, and update it later if needed. The seller's average rating is shown on their profile and on their active listings.
