General

Platform overview, language settings, reporting issues, and contacting the team.

Console Notebook is a community platform dedicated to gaming hardware. It brings together an encyclopedia of 52 consoles (generations 1–9, 1972–2025), a community forum, a peer-to-peer marketplace, a repair wizard, and social features (friends, chat, profiles, achievements) — all in one place.

The platform is built for collectors, retro gaming enthusiasts, and anyone who wants to learn about, compare, or discuss gaming hardware.
Most content is accessible without an account:
  1. •
    Browse the full console encyclopedia (all 52 pages).
  2. •
    Use the comparison tool.
  3. •
    Read the Learn section.
An account is required to post in the forum, send messages, submit a repair request, rate or favourite consoles, add items to the marketplace, or track your progress.
Console Notebook currently supports two languages:
  1. •
    English — the default language.
  2. •
    Romanian — full interface translation.
  3. •
    Italian — full interface translation.
  4. •
    Spanish — full interface translation.
  5. •
    French — full interface translation.
  6. •
    German — full interface translation.
The platform automatically uses your browser's preferred language on first visit. You can change the language at any time using the language switcher in the page footer.
To change the interface language you can use the language switcher located in the profile dropdown menu in the top right corner of the page.
💡 User-generated content (forum posts, marketplace listings, chat messages) is always displayed in the language it was originally written in.
You can report bugs or technical issues to the team through the following channel:
  1. •
    Contact form — go to Help & Support → Contact and send a direct message to the team.
When reporting a bug, include:
  1. •
    A description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  2. •
    The page or feature where the issue occurred.
  3. •
    Your browser and operating system.
  4. •
    Any error messages you saw.
You can reach the team through the following channels:
  1. •
    Contact form — on the Help & Support page, scroll to the Contact section and fill in the form. Your message is delivered directly to the team's inbox.
  2. •
    Email — console.notebook.app@gmail.com
  3. •
    Phone — +40 758 464 907
💡 For repair-related questions, use the Repair Wizard instead of the contact form — it ensures your request is tracked and routed correctly.
Look for the share button — on your public profile, next to an unlocked achievement or your level card on the Stats page, and on marketplace listings. On mobile or supported browsers it opens your device's native share sheet; otherwise it copies a link to your clipboard.
