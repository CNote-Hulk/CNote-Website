Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  consolenotebook.com

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed by the site on your device (computer, phone, tablet). They allow the site to remember information between visits — your preferences, login session, or anonymous statistical data. Console Notebook uses both cookies and similar technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage, which are subject to the same legal rules (ePrivacy + GDPR).

2. Types used on consolenotebook.com

We use two main categories: strictly necessary (always active, no consent required — legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and analytics (optional, only with consent — legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

3. Strictly necessary cookies and storage

These items are essential for the Platform to operate and cannot be disabled without disrupting the service. They store your interface preferences, login session, and consent choice.

cn_session · HttpOnly cookie · session duration · purpose: maintaining authentication.

cn_cookie_consent · localStorage · persistent · purpose: storing your cookie choice.

cnote-theme · localStorage · persistent · purpose: visual theme (light/dark/sepia).

cnote-accent-color · localStorage · persistent · purpose: custom accent colour.

cnote_lang · localStorage · persistent · purpose: interface language (RO/EN/DE/ES/FR/IT).

4. Analytics cookies (optional)

These cookies are active only if you click "Accept All" in the cookie banner. They are set by Google Analytics 4 (ID: G-Q07J9XCMJP) and collect pseudonymised data about site usage.

_ga · third-party cookie (google-analytics.com) · 2 years · purpose: unique visitor identifier (pseudonym).

_ga_Q07J9XCMJP · third-party cookie · 2 years · purpose: GA4 session state for consolenotebook.com.

Data collected: pages visited, time on page, traffic source, device, browser, approximate country. IP addresses are anonymised under GA4 EU settings.

5. Third-party cookies (optional)

When you enable optional cookies, certain resources are loaded from third-party servers, which may set their own cookies:

— YouTube (embedded videos on the courses page): VISITOR_INFO1_LIVE, YSC, PREF · variable duration · policies.google.com/privacy;

— Google Analytics (when consent is granted): see above.

Google Fonts and the Socket.IO CDN (cdn.socket.io) are loaded on every page for basic site functionality and do not set tracking cookies.

6. How we obtain and store consent

On your first visit, a banner appears with Accept All or Decline options. Your decision is stored locally in cn_cookie_consent and can be changed at any time via the settings panel below or by clearing localStorage in your browser. Declining does not affect basic Platform functionality.

7. Your preferences

You can manage your cookie preferences below. Changes take effect immediately on save.

8. Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete or block cookies directly from your browser. Note: disabling strictly necessary cookies will disrupt normal site functionality.

— Chrome · Firefox · Safari · Edge.
