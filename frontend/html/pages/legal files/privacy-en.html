Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  consolenotebook.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Console Notebook ("Platform") collects, uses and protects your personal data, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Romanian Law no. 190/2018 and applicable national law.

2. Data controller

Andrei Halcu, natural person, jurisdiction Romania, is the controller of your personal data.

Contact: console.notebook.app@gmail.com · +40 758 464 907

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed as the criteria of GDPR Art. 37 are not met. For any data-related request, please use the email above.

3. Data we collect

a) Account identification data: username, email address, password (stored only as a bcrypt hash), avatar, bio.

b) Authentication data: JWT tokens, session ID, refresh token, IP at login, user-agent, timestamp.

c) Google OAuth data (optional): if you choose Google sign-in — Google ID, email, name, profile picture.

d) User-generated content: forum posts, chat messages, direct messages, marketplace listings, repair requests, ratings, favourite/owned console lists, achievements, course/lesson progress.

e) Relational data: friend list, blocks, reports sent/received.

f) Technical data: IP address, user-agent, request timestamps, server logs (Railway), error logs (Sentry).

g) Communication data: messages sent via the contact form and email.

h) Analytics data (optional, with consent only): Google Analytics 4 collects pseudonymised data about visited pages, session duration, traffic source, device, country.

4. Purposes and legal bases

— Service delivery (account creation, authentication, profile, UGC, marketplace, chat, forum, courses, gamification). Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of the contract represented by the Terms.

— Security, fraud prevention, technical logs, DSA mechanism. Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest to maintain the integrity of the Platform.

— Transactional emails (account verification, password reset, critical account notifications). Basis: Art. 6(1)(b).

— In-app notifications (forum replies, incoming messages, achievements). Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or your notification preferences.

— Site analytics (GA4) and embedded YouTube videos. Basis: Art. 6(1)(a) — consent, given via the cookie banner.

— Compliance with legal obligations (log retention for authority requests, DSA, GDPR). Basis: Art. 6(1)(c) — legal obligation.

6. International transfers

Some processors (Google, Sentry, Railway) may transfer data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), notably to the US. Such transfers rely on:

— EU-US "Data Privacy Framework" adequacy decision;
— Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) adopted by the European Commission;
— Additional technical measures (encryption at rest and in transit).

7. Retention

— Active account data: for the lifetime of the account.
— After account deletion: personal data is deleted within 30 days. Forum/chat messages may be anonymised (username replaced with "[deleted]") to preserve conversation coherence.
— Technical and security logs: 90 days.
— DSA and moderation reports: 6 months from the final decision (DSA Art. 17).
— GA4 analytics data: Google's default settings (max. 14 months, pseudonymised).
— Accounting/legal records (if transactions occur): 10 years per the Romanian Fiscal Code.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: HTTPS/TLS encryption, bcrypt password hashing, signed JWTs, Helmet HTTP headers, rate-limiting, strict CORS, Sentry for early error detection, automated database backups, least-privilege access control.

In the event of a breach posing risk to your rights, we will notify ANSPDCP within 72 hours and inform you directly under Art. 33–34 GDPR.

9. Your rights (Art. 15–22 GDPR)

— Access to your data and a copy thereof;
— Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
— Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — from settings or by email;
— Restriction of processing in legally defined cases;
— Portability — export your data in a structured format (JSON);
— Objection to processing based on legitimate interest;
— Consent withdrawal at any time (cookie settings or email), without affecting prior lawful processing;
— Complaint to ANSPDCP (Romanian Data Protection Authority), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, Bucharest, dataprotection.ro. EU residents may also lodge complaints with their local supervisory authority.

To exercise rights: console.notebook.app@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days (extendable by 60 days in complex cases).

10. Minors

The Platform is intended for users aged 16 or above. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under that age. If we become aware of, or are notified about, a minor's account, the account will be suspended and the data deleted.

11. Automated decisions and profiling

We do not use automated decision-making producing legal effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR). The gamification system (XP, achievements, levels) is purely algorithmic and based solely on your visible Platform activity.

We may update this Policy. Substantial changes will be notified by email and/or a visible banner on the Platform, at least 15 days before taking effect. The last-updated date appears at the top of this document.
