Community Rules

Last updated: May 2026  ·  consolenotebook.com

1. Scope

These Rules supplement the Terms and Conditions and set out the behavioural standards in all community spaces: forum, general chat, direct messages (DMs), console comments, marketplace listings, repair requests, profile bio, ratings. They apply to all registered users.

2. General principles

— Respect: treat others as you would like to be treated.
— On topic: Console Notebook is a platform about video game consoles — keep discussions relevant.
— Authentic: express your own views, do not copy content from elsewhere.
— Constructive: disagreement is allowed, personal attacks are not.

3. Prohibited content and conduct

a) Illegal content: any form of incitement to hatred, discrimination, violence, terrorism, sexual exploitation of minors, CSAM, death threats, defamation. Such content is reported to the competent authorities.

b) Harassment: repeated insults, doxxing (publishing someone's personal data), stalking, unwanted messages after being blocked.

c) NSFW / pornographic content: sexually explicit imagery, extreme violence, graphic gore.

d) Spam: repetitive messages, unauthorised commercial advertising, pyramid schemes, "follow-for-follow", abusive referrals.

e) Piracy: links to pirated BIOSes, ROMs, games or software, license keys, cracks. Legal modding (your own hardware) is allowed in the dedicated section.

f) Fraud: scams, phishing, "too good to be true" schemes, counterfeit products, identity fraud.

g) Manipulation: multiple accounts, fake votes, rating manipulation, artificial boosting of achievements/XP.

h) Security: hacking attempts, exploiting bugs instead of reporting them, distributing malware/suspicious links.

i) Commercial promotion: advertising your own businesses or paid services without prior approval. Pricing discussions are allowed in the marketplace.

4. Forum-specific rules

— Check if your topic has been discussed before starting a new thread.
— Use descriptive titles (NOT "Help" or "Question").
— Post in the correct category (PlayStation / Xbox / Nintendo / Sega / Off-topic, etc.).
— Avoid necroposting (replying to posts older than 6 months unless you add something genuinely new).
— Spoilers for games must be clearly marked.

5. Chat and direct message rules

— The general chat is for everyone — keep a friendly tone.
— Do not flood the chat (mass messages, repeated emoji, CAPS LOCK).
— Use DMs for long 1-on-1 discussions.
— DMs are not proactively monitored, but illegal content can be reported via the ⚑ button in the message UI.
— Do not send unsolicited commercial DMs.

6. Marketplace rules

— List only real consoles and accessories you own or sell in your own name.
— Describe the condition accurately: new / used / non-functional / for parts.
— Add real photos (not Google stock images).
— Specify price and currency — do not accept upfront payments without protection (Wise, PayPal buyer protection, in-person meeting).
— Do not repost the same listing within short intervals to push it up the feed.
— Mark a listing as sold/withdrawn when the product is no longer available.
— Trading counterfeit products, pre-loaded ROMs, or hardware modded for piracy is PROHIBITED.

7. Repair requests

— Describe the symptom clearly: what the console does / doesn't do, when it started, what you have already tried.
— Add the exact model, board photos if relevant.
— Those who answer are volunteers — there is no guarantee of a solution.
— Repairs are at your own risk — see warnings in Terms, Courses section.
— Do not request ROMs or piracy-related solutions as part of repair.

8. How to report content or users

On every post, listing, public message or profile you will find the ⚑ Report button. Choose the reason (spam, hate speech, illegal content, fraud, etc.) and optionally add context. For abusive DMs, use the ⚑ button inside the conversation. False or repeated reports may attract sanctions.

For reporting illegal content under DSA Art. 16, see the full procedure in Terms, DSA section.

9. Sanctions and enforcement ladder

Sanctions are applied according to severity, frequency and intent:

— Warning (minor breach, first time): in-app and email notification.
— Content removal: the post/listing/message is taken down; retained in logs for appeal.
— Temporary mute (1-72 hours): cannot post or message.
— Account suspension (3-30 days): access limited to read-only.
— Permanent ban: reserved for severe breaches (CSAM, scams, severe hate speech, recidivism after multiple sanctions).

Illegal content is removed immediately and reported to the competent authorities as required by law.

10. Appeals and internal complaint mechanism (DSA Art. 20)

If you consider a sanction unfair, you may appeal within 6 months of receiving the notice. Send a motivated request to console.notebook.app@gmail.com, including: account ID, the content concerned, reason for the appeal, evidence (if any).

You will receive a reasoned reply within 14 days. In case of rejection, you may use an independent out-of-court dispute settlement body (DSA Art. 21) or the competent courts.

11. Moderation contact

For questions about the Rules or moderation matters: console.notebook.app@gmail.com (subject: "Moderation — [issue]").
