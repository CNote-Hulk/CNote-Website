Platforma acoperă console din toate generațiile: de la Atari 2600 (1977) până la PlayStation 5 și Xbox Series X (2020), inclusiv console portabile (Game Boy, PSP, Nintendo DS), console rare și hardware obscur. Fiecare consolă are specificații tehnice complete și istoric detaliat.
Every piece of user-generated content has a ⚑ Report button — on forum posts, marketplace listings, user profiles, and direct messages. Select a reason (spam, hate speech, harassment, illegal content, CSAM, misinformation, or other), add an optional description, and submit. You can track the status of your reports in Settings → My Reports. This system complies with DSA Article 16.
XP is earned automatically from your activity: completing lessons (50 XP), finishing a course (200 XP), visiting console pages (10 XP), posting in the forum (20 XP), adding friends (15 XP), creating marketplace listings (15 XP), and unlocking achievements (20–500 XP). There are 10 levels from 🔌 Newcomer (0 XP) to 👑 Legend (13 000 XP). See the Achievements & Progress guide for the full list.
